| 修复项 | 修复前 | 修复后 |

| 业务逻辑 | — | 零变化（仅 BOM 与变量界定） |

修复命令（一行，已执行）：

```powershell
$path = 'D:\AWork\TraeAdmin\VRSanguoYanWuchang\Set-TraeGovernanceAcl.ps1'; $utf8Bom = [System.Text.UTF8Encoding]::new($true); $content = [System.IO.File]::ReadAllText($path, $utf8Bom); $content = $content -replace '"\$currentUser:', '"${currentUser}:' -replace '"\$systemAccount:', '"${systemAccount}:' -replace '"\$administrators:', '"${administrators}:' -replace '"\$users:', '"${users}:'; [System.IO.File]::WriteAllText($path, $content, $utf8Bom)
```
